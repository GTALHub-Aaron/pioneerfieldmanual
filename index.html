Where to, pioneer?

PlannerLay out production lines, balance them, and build whole chains back to raw ore. MapResource nodes and collectibles from your own game files. Add a miner straight from a node. WikiEvery item, building, recipe and unlock, linked to the planner and the map.

To open a section directly, add #/planner, #/map or #/wiki to the end of the planner’s address, for example on a Stream Deck button.

Game progress

The planner only uses what you have. Locked buildings disappear from the recipe list and Build chain won't add them. Wires that need a faster belt or pipe than you have show how many to run side by side. Update this as you progress through the tiers.

Logistics

A miner or machine pushes each item out through one belt or pipe, so none of them is planned faster than your fastest one can carry. A Mk.3 pure miner on 3 shards with Mk.5 belts is planned at 780/min, and its card tells you the clock speed to set.

Extraction

Production amplifier

Smelters and Constructors take 1; Assemblers, Foundries, Refineries and Converters 2; Manufacturers, Blenders, Particle Accelerators and Quantum Encoders 4. Packagers take none. Filling every slot doubles output from the same inputs and uses 4× power; it stacks with overclocking. Miners and extractors can’t take them.

How the Field Manual works

  1. Set your progress. Open Game progress (top left) and tick what you've unlocked: best belt and pipe, miner tier, power shards, buildings, alternate recipes and Somersloops. The planner only uses what you have.
  2. Add what you want to make. Find a recipe in the left panel (search or open a building group) and drag it onto the grid, or click it.
  3. Click Build chain on that card. Every machine it needs is added and wired back to raw resources, reusing machines you already have.
  4. Set a target. The last card in a line is an end product. Give it an Exact amount per minute or a Share of leftover.
  5. Size it. Balance to targets sizes everything to hit your numbers. Max from supply makes as much as your miners allow.
  6. Build it in game. Each card says how many machines to place, the clock speed, shards and Somersloops. Open Belt layout on a card for splitter and merger steps.
End products
Any card whose main output isn't wired onward is an end product, with Target controls on the card.
Exact amount
A fixed rate per minute. With several exact targets, Priority 1 is filled first.
Share of leftover
Splits whatever supply is left after exact targets, by quantity. 100% and 100% means equal amounts; 200% gets twice as much.
Balance to targets
Sizes every machine and miner to exactly meet your targets.
Max from supply
Keeps your miners and oil as they are and grows or shrinks everything to use them fully. Water extractors are added as needed, since water is unlimited.
Fit
Sizes one card to what's connected after it.
Totals panel
On the right: targets met or short, raw extraction, unsupplied inputs, excess output, power and building counts.
Shortfalls
The header count of items that aren't fully supplied. Inputs turn amber or red on cards, and short wires go dashed.
Machines
The workload, counted in machines at 100%. The grey line at the bottom turns it into what to build, like "Build 3 @ 83.3%".
Power shards & Somersloops
Click the round lights to set how many each machine gets. Shards allow overclocking; Somersloops boost output and use more power.
Recipe
Switch between the standard recipe and any alternates you've unlocked. Wires that still fit are kept.
Main menu & wiki
Click FICSIT or the house button for the main menu. The Wiki covers every item, building, recipe, milestone and MAM research, with links between them. + Planner adds a recipe as a card, Plan it builds a whole chain, and Show on map filters the map to that resource. To open a section directly, add #/planner, #/map or #/wiki to the planner's address.
Wiki tools
The wiki has calculators (overclocking, Somersloops, storage), AWESOME Sink, Power & fuels and Belts & pipes pages. Hover any link for a quick preview, star pages to keep them under Favourites, and tick Only what I can make now to hide what you haven’t unlocked.
Map
Switch between Grid and Map at the top, or press M. The map uses files from your own game: export the map picture and the level data with FModel, then use Import FModel export and Load map picture in the map's left panel. Or put map.png and a saved map-data.js in the icons folder. Filter nodes by resource and purity, click one for details, and Add Miner to this plan to make a card linked to that node. Linked cards get an On map button, and nodes this plan uses get an orange ring.
Base on one resource
Type the total you get into a miner or extractor card’s Output /min, like 2100 m³ of Nitrogen Gas, and it adds the extractors needed to deliver exactly that. Then On a miner or extractor card, Base on this on that card keeps it exactly as it is and scales everything it feeds so its whole output gets used. Every other miner, extractor and machine in the line resizes to match, so you can see what else you'd need. Handy when one resource, like Nitrogen Gas, is the limit.
Recipe choice
The ▾ next to Build chain sets which recipes it picks: standard, Fewest raw resources, Fewest buildings or Least power, using only alternates you've unlocked. Optimize this chain re-picks recipes for every machine feeding that card, removes machines no longer needed and wires in new ones. The ▾ turns orange when a rule other than standard is on.
Miners & extractors
Set resource, tier, node purity and count. Max /min caps output; blank means your fastest belt or pipe.
Generators
Pick a fuel and set the MW you want. The header shows power made, used and net.
Belt layout
How to split belts into the machines and merge them out, including what to do when the count doesn't split evenly. Draw layout opens a to-scale drawing on an 8 m foundation grid: drag machines and belt lines, pick which side each feed comes in from, and read off the splitters, mergers, belt lengths and foundations you need. Colour the belts and pipes by material or by Mk level; pipes are drawn hollow so they stand out where they cross belts. Whole line draws every card in the line as its own block; drag blocks around and the belts between them follow, and double-click one to arrange its machines. Your layout is saved with the plan.
Ports
Inputs sit above outputs and reorder themselves so wires don't cross.
Wiring
Drag from a port to a matching port. Drop a wire on empty grid to pick a machine that makes or uses that item.
Wire colours
Plum for ore, grey for ingots, orange for parts, teal for fluids. Amber dashed means short; a red label means more than one belt can carry.
Wire labels
Flow per minute and the belt or pipe needed. Hover a label to highlight its wire; click it to select, then Delete to remove.
Tidy layout
Arranges cards left to right from raw resources to products. Auto-tidy does it whenever Build chain adds cards; Spacing sets how tight.
Selecting
Click a card header. Shift-click or Shift-drag on empty grid to select several. The toolbar middle then offers copy, duplicate, Build chain and delete.
Find
The search box at the top left of the grid zooms to a card by name.
Moving around
Drag empty grid to pan, scroll to zoom, Fit view to see everything.
Plans
Name and save plans, open or delete them. Once named, a plan saves itself as you work.
Sharing
In Plans, Share a plan as a file or text copies a plan or saves a file someone else can open in their planner.
Game progress
What you've unlocked. Locked buildings and recipes are hidden, and nothing is planned faster than your best belt.
New card defaults
At the top of the left panel: tier, shards, purity and Somersloops for cards you add. Apply to all updates existing cards.
Settings
Icons, wire animation, wire labels, number detail, toolbar tips and the Clear confirmation.
Icons
Put your exported icon folder next to the planner and name it icons, or load it from Settings. Click any icon to see it larger.
